# Changelog

## 0.8.2 — Acts III and IV, dressed

**Interiors**
- 16 new rendered props: palace tapestries, banquet tables, a throne on its dais, suits of armour, bookcases, carpet
  runners, statues of saints, altars, rows of pews, stained-glass walls, knights' tomb effigies, the stake and its
  faggots, a scaffold, wall shackles, reliquaries and a font.
- The Legate's palace, the royal palace and the papal palace are hung with tapestries and lined with armour,
  bookcases and pillars, with banquet tables and carpets on the floor.
- The underground cathedral has pews, tombs, saints and reliquaries among the bones. Notre-Dame is ringed with
  stained glass and pillars over rows of pews.
- The Tower of Truth is chains, cells, cages and the rack, floor after floor.
- Each of these levels now carries 100 to 220 pieces where it had only candelabra and braziers.

**Set pieces behind the great fights**
- A throne on its dais behind the royal palace's last guardian and behind the Pope, with an altar and a reliquary.
  An altar between stained-glass windows in Notre-Dame. The stake, a scaffold and a banner on the Place de Grève.
  A throne and tapestries for the Legate. An altar and tombs in the deepest cathedral. Shackles in the top of the Tower.

**Paris and the camps**
- The Place de Grève is lined with townhouses, stalls and torch posts, with gibbets, stocks, a prison wagon and an
  edict board in the square. The Remnant camp gains weapon racks, stores and a cart. The Monastery of Trials gains
  lecterns, saints and the stocks.

## 0.8.1 — Act II, dressed

**The Town in Shadow**
- The town now has streets: rows of timber-framed townhouses with lit windows and jettied upper floors, shops with
  awnings, houses boarded up and marked by the Inquisition, an edict board, a prison wagon, the stocks and a gibbet in
  the square, torch posts along the lanes and banners at the north gate. The lanes to every NPC, exit and the
  waypoint stay clear, and nothing tall stands in front of the exits.
- 5 new rendered buildings and props: townhouse, merchant's house, boarded house, notice board, prison wagon.

**Underground**
- 16 new rendered props for the catacombs, the Hermit's cave and the Inquisition's house: ossuary walls stacked
  with bones, coffins, stalagmites, glowing cave fungus, rubble, mine timbers with hanging lanterns, wine casks,
  smugglers' caches, gibbet cages, the rack, the stocks, cell bars, Inquisition banners, lecterns, puddles and pillars.
- Each underground zone has its own set: smugglers' stores in the first catacombs, bone halls deeper down, a
  fungus-lit cave, cells and instruments in the Inquisition's house. About 2 to 3 times as much dressing as before
  (120 to 250 pieces a level), and the Abandoned Castle gains rubble, pillars and a gibbet.
- Hand-placed decor now keeps the automatic dressing off its whole footprint, so nothing spawns inside a house.

**Art**
- La Tarasque's spines re-cut: thinner, in rings along the shell, no longer a thatch.

## 0.8.0 — the Order's endgame

**Gear**
- Fourteen equipment slots: helm, shoulders, chest, mantle, gauntlets and bracers, belt, legs, boots, two rings,
  amulet, pack, weapon and shield. 27 new base items, each with its own rendered icon. A bigger pack carries more.
- The bag is now a paper doll and an icon grid, with full tooltips (properties with runes and traits, sockets, set
  bonuses), drag and drop and right-click quick actions.
- Rarities: Exotic and Mythic join the top. Only one of each may be worn; they grow by swallowing another piece of
  the same rarity at the forge (empowering, up to rank 10). Mythic pieces carry their own names (Durendal, Joyeuse...).
- Item sets: six sets with 2-, 4- and 6-piece bonuses. Exalted pieces (Proving Pit floors 6 and deeper) have a gold
  name and one more bonus line. The Book of Deeds (J) records every piece you have held; finishing a set pays.
- Traits: weapons, armour and jewellery can be keen, balanced, blessed, reinforced, padded, gilded and more.
  Research a trait by taking an item apart; then put it on any piece of the family with Holy Oil.

**Runes**
- Sockets in weapons and armour (chest, legs and two-handers 3; shoulders and mantles 2; most others 1; Exotic and
  Mythic one more). 13 kinds of rune. Take a rune out with one click, anywhere.
- Every rune drops at Common tier, level 1, and grows with the kills made while it is worn and the blows it strikes
  or takes, to level 50. Two at 50 of one kind and tier fuse at the forge into the next tier. Higher tiers only from
  world bosses and the Iron King.

**The forge room**
- Master Aubry's forge has its own room under the Sanctuary (west door): anvil, hearth, quench tub. Tempering
  (+1 to +10, with a risk of shattering past +4), salvage, research, traits, empowering, and the old combining.
- Gilles the Runecarver fuses runes. The Athanor (the alchemists' furnace) turns four items into one, four
  Legendaries and Soul Cores into an Exotic, and an Exotic with Legendaries, Soul Cores and a Trophy Relic into a
  Mythic.
- Every piece of forge work plays a cutscene: three hammer blows in a shower of sparks, then a burst of light in the
  rarity's colour, or a cloud of smoke when the steel breaks.

**The world**
- The Grey Mist: every hour one zone is taken over. Monsters are stronger and drop Mist Embers; three Mist Coffers
  open for embers; a threat meter fills as you kill, and when it is full the Grey Huntsman and his hounds come for you.
- The Proving Pit (Sanctuary, south-east door): ten floors of rising waves with champions on floors 5 and 10, Soul
  Cores for every floor.
- La Tarasque, the world boss: muster at Tarascon through Guillaume the Herald; when the horn sounds up to twenty
  knights cross to the shore together. Her blows are shared between everyone there, and only there can the fallen
  be revived by their brothers. She drops Trophy Relics, Menagerie Tokens, high runes and Exotic and Mythic pieces.
- Relic arts: Trophy Relics teach Cleave, Riposte, War Cry and Crusader's Charge.
- Daily pledges and endeavours from Brother Almoner, paid in upgrade materials. Two weekly instabilities change how
  the Mist and the Pit fight.
- Level sync: in a zone below you, you fight at its level (+2), with your gear scaled to match.
- Armoury loadouts: your own cell off the Sanctuary (and every guild hall) has an armoury rack; save up to four
  harnesses and change into one in a single step.
- Companions: six to buy from Mathilde of the Menagerie, with gold and Menagerie Tokens. They follow you everywhere.

**Engine**
- Protocol version 9 (items carry sockets, traits, sets and upgrades; snapshots carry pets): old clients must update.
- Zones can hold more players (world bosses 20), be personal (one copy per knight), or be the Proving Pit.

## 0.7.0 — the living world

**World**
- 19 new kinds of scenery, built and rendered by our own Blender scripts: oaks, pines, dead and burnt trees,
  boulders, rock piles, bushes, stumps, fallen logs, graves, braziers, torch posts, striped pavilions, Templar
  banners, market stalls, grass and wild flowers.
- Every outdoor zone is dressed for its land. The forests are thick with trees, the fields and roads have hedges and
  copses, the vineyards have frost-killed trees, the quarry has boulders, the burned village has charred trunks, the
  drowned commandery has dead trees and graves, the camp has tents and fires, and the market squares have stalls.
  Exits, NPCs, waypoints and the ways between them always stay clear.
- Large halls and towns get braziers and torch posts that light the ground and throw embers.
- Trees and other tall scenery turn see-through while your hero is behind them.
- The map (Tab) and minimap show forests, rocks, buildings and fires.

**Heroes**
- The knight shows the weapon family he has equipped: sword and shield, dagger and shield, two-handed sword,
  maul or club, or throwing axe and shield. Other players see it too.

**Sound**
- Every zone has its own ambience: wind, biting wind in the frost, rain, a crypt's drips, birdsong in the forests,
  crickets and campfires at night, bells over the towns, the hush of great halls.
- Standing near a brazier, a candelabrum or a fire brings up its crackle.
- New hit, critical, block, swing, death and spell sounds, and your own footsteps.

**Engine**
- Player snapshots carry the equipped weapon family (protocol version 8: old clients must update).
- Ambience layers in the audio engine (looping, cross-fading, distance-driven).

## 0.6.0 — light and fire

- A new rendering pipeline in the Taco Factory engine:
  - dynamic lights, bloom and colour grading;
  - vignette, film grain, and a red pulse at the screen edges when you are badly hurt.
- Every zone has its own light level, colour and weather: embers, ash, snow, rain, spores, fireflies and dust motes.
- Candelabra light the crypts and halls. Fires burn in the courtyard at Payns, the camp and the Place de Grève.
- Combat effects:
  - sparks, blood that stains the ground, and impact flashes;
  - screen shake, and a brief hit-stop on critical hits and kills;
  - spells in their school's colours: bolts with trails, bursts, novas, auras, drains and blinks;
  - a golden pillar on level-up, a pillar of light over rare drops, and boss slams that shake the ground.
- The camera follows smoothly and leads in the direction you walk. Damage numbers pop and drift.
- Settings → Lighting and effects turns all of it off for older graphics cards.
- Fixed: every figure was drawn mirrored left to right, so heroes and monsters seemed to walk backwards.
- The demo opens every waypoint and puts one of every weapon and armour piece in the strongbox.
