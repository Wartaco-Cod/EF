# Story, choices and endings

The story follows the plan's storyboard: from the night of the arrests at Payns to one of four endings. It is
all data: towns, zones and NPCs in `assets/data/zones`, quests in `quests.json`, scenes in `cutscenes.json`,
boss fights and choices in `bosses.json`. Acts II-IV are generated by `tools/content/acts.py`; edit the tables
there and run it again rather than editing the JSON by hand.

Every town has a quest giver, merchant, healer, smith (once Aubry's forge is lit), strongbox, auction broker and
an arena for duels. Each act's last main quest opens the road to the next town; waypoints in every act are
shown on their own tab.

## Choices

Three bosses yield instead of dying: **Captain Réginald** (Royal Garrison), **Sir Arnaud de Foix** (Underground
Cathedral) and **Pope Clement V** (Papal Palace). The party then has 30 seconds to vote. The majority decides
what happens in the world (a tie goes to the party leader): spared bosses walk away and drop only their
guaranteed item; killed ones drop everything. **Each player keeps the choice they voted for** in their own story.

Other story marks: rescuing Brother Thomas (Act I) earns his trust; the optional Legate's Vault (Act III)
secures the proof for de Molay's defence; retaking the castle and the Hermit's secret count too.

## Allies

In the Place de Grève, the Remnant marches with you: two Brothers of the Remnant, plus one more for each of
Thomas's trust, Réginald spared, Arnaud spared, the castle retaken and the Hermit's secret, held by anyone in
the party. Spared characters also appear in the Remnant's Camp.

## Disguise

Marie gives you cleric's robes after the King's Forest. In the Royal Court, guards ignore a disguised player
until they are within a few paces for a second and a half, or until the player strikes anything. Then the
disguise breaks and every guard nearby comes running. Court hounds are not fooled at all.

## Endings

Decided from each player's own choices when Philip falls:

| Ending | When |
| --- | --- |
| The Darkness | Réginald, Arnaud and Clement were all put to death |
| The Rescue | The proof was secured, Thomas trusts you, and at most one of the three was killed |
| The Vengeance | Clement was condemned (and the Rescue's conditions were not met) |
| The Redemption | Otherwise |

## Decisions made while building (check these)

- Act II's towns and zones follow the plan doc's World design (the story bible), not the older prototype data.
- The plan's level bands are used as written, so the game runs 1-100 across the four acts; no replayed difficulty tiers.
- Dungeons the plan gives several floors are separate zones ("Floor 2 of 3"); the King's Forest and Underground Cathedral are split in two.
- Names the plan does not give were invented: the Town in Shadow's farrier Odette, the brokers Lucas and Mathilde, Isabeau the clothier, Renier the armourer, and the uniques (Bastien the Ferryman, Brother Guichard, Hugues le Routier, the Pale Thing in the Well, the Warden of the Cells, the Master of the Royal Hunt, the Black Boar of Vincennes, Prior Anselme, the Keeper of the Rolls, the Ossuary Abbot, the Keeper of the Vault, the Provost of Paris, the Captain of the Royal Guard, the Cardinal's Champion, the Gaoler of the Tower, the Living Confession). Nogaret and Marigny are the historical figures.
- The Act IV hub is the plan's proposed Remnant's Camp.
- Cutscenes are text over a picture for now; the party's choices are a vote with the leader breaking ties, as the plan describes.
- The spell field names, skill costs and the Paladin's aura are unchanged from before.
